MyDoughnut Privacy Policy

Live well. Spend wisely. Protect the planet.

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version: MyDoughnut keeps everything you enter, and anything it reads from Apple Health, on your own device. We don't run servers that receive your data, we don't use analytics or advertising, and we never sell or share your information.

1. Who we are

MyDoughnut is published by Adnan Altimeemy, Manchester, M19 2PT, United Kingdom. For the purposes of UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), we are responsible for this policy. You can contact us at adnanaltimeemy@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores, and where

All of the information below is stored only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app's private storage and an App Group container shared with MyDoughnut's own widgets. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

InformationWhy
Your first name, household size, weekly spending and carbon budgetsTo personalise the app and compare your week against your own targets
Daily check-in ratings and notesTo calculate your doughnut scores
Habits, challenge progress, badges and levelsTo track your progress and streaks
Purchases you log (description, amount, category, date)To show the cost and estimated carbon impact of your spending

3. Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, MyDoughnut asks for read-only access to: steps, exercise minutes, walking and running distance, cycling distance, sleep and mindful minutes. You can allow or deny each type individually, and change your mind at any time in the Health app (Sharing → Apps → MyDoughnut) or iOS Settings.

  • We use this data only to calculate your Physical health, Mental wellbeing and Transport scores, and to show 7-day averages in the app and its widgets.
  • MyDoughnut never writes data to Apple Health.
  • Health data stays on your device. It is never sent to us, never stored in iCloud by the app, and never shared with third parties.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and is never sold.

4. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created on your device. No push-notification service or server is involved. You can turn them off in the app (Profile → Notifications) or in iOS Settings.

5. What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information.

Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics (such as download counts) under Apple's own privacy policy. These cannot identify you.

6. Legal basis

Because your information stays on your device and we don't receive it, we don't process it as a data controller in the usual sense. Where UK data protection law applies, the app processes your data on the basis of your consent (for Apple Health access and notifications) and to provide the features you ask for.

7. How long data is kept

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase everything at any time with Profile → Reset all data. Deleting the app also deletes all of its data from your device. If you use iPhone or iPad backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), your app data may be included in those backups, which are controlled by you and Apple.

8. Your rights

You are in full control of your information on your device: you can view, edit and delete it in the app at any time. Under UK data protection law you also have rights to access, correct, delete and restrict processing of personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to provide, but you can contact us with any question. If you're unhappy with how we handle a privacy concern, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

9. Children

MyDoughnut is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly receive information from children.

10. Estimates, not advice

Carbon figures are rough estimates to help you compare choices. MyDoughnut does not provide medical, financial or legal advice.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data (for example, adding optional iCloud sync or a family-sharing feature), we will update this page and its "Last updated" date before the change ships, and highlight significant changes in the app's release notes.
